Who I Am

I'm an aspiring cybersecurity engineer with 3+ years of experience as a Security Operations Center analyst, currently working as a SOC Analyst L1 at eir evo in Dublin, Ireland. My day-to-day involves monitoring and triaging security events, tuning detection rules to reduce false positives, and leading root-cause investigations with SIEM platforms including IBM QRadar, Splunk, and Microsoft Sentinel.

I hold an MSc in Cybersecurity from the National College of Ireland, where my thesis focused on detecting malicious ("mal-adware") URLs. Before that, I worked as a penetration testing and security engineering intern, running OWASP Top 10-aligned assessments and supporting SIEM operations for a police department.

I rank in the top 1% on TryHackMe, hold CompTIA Security+ and SOC Level 1 certification, and I'm continually adding to that list — most recently exploring agentic AI and its implications for security operations. Outside of work, I mentor early-career students moving into tech.
